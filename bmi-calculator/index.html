BMI is a general screening tool and doesn't diagnose health status. It doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, or body composition. For personal medical advice, please consult a healthcare professional in person.

Indian-adjusted (ICMR) BMI ranges

CategoryBMI (kg/m²)
UnderweightBelow 18.5
Normal weight18.5 – 22.9
Overweight23 – 24.9
Obese25 and above

These are narrower than the generic WHO ranges (which use 25 and 30 as cutoffs) because research shows Indians tend to develop diabetes and heart disease at lower BMI levels than European populations.
