How It Works

Your first four visits, explained

No app, no video call, no guessing. Here's exactly what happens when you start at OBO Health — from your first exam to ongoing, doctor-led care.

  1. Visit 1 — In-person exam & full diagnostics

    You come into the JM Road clinic and are examined by your doctor in person — never over a video call. We run a comprehensive blood panel (96 parameters) to understand your metabolic health, not just your weight.

  2. Visit 1 — Your doctor reviews results with you

    Your doctor explains your labs in plain language, discusses your goals, and decides — based on your actual exam and results — whether medication is appropriate, and at what starting dose.

  3. Weekly visits — dose titration, in person

    If medication is prescribed, your dose is increased gradually and reviewed at each visit by your doctor, alongside nutrition and lifestyle coaching — not an app deciding your dose for you.

  4. Between visits — WhatsApp support (scheduling only)

    Reminders, scheduling and check-ins happen over WhatsApp for convenience. This assistant never diagnoses or prescribes — it exists to support your doctor's care, not replace it.

  5. Ongoing — monthly labs & maintenance

    As you progress, lab frequency and visit cadence adjust (see your plan details) — your doctor continues to monitor and adjust your treatment for as long as you're with us.
