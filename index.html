What to expect

No app, no video call, no guessing. Here's exactly what happens when you start at OBO Health.

  1. Visit 1 — In-person exam & full diagnostics

    You come into the JM Road clinic and are examined by your doctor in person — never over a video call. We run a comprehensive blood panel (96 parameters) to understand your metabolic health, not just your weight.

  2. Visit 2 — Your doctor reviews results with you

    Your doctor explains your labs in plain language, discusses your goals, and decides — based on your actual exam and results — whether medication is appropriate, and at what starting dose.

  3. Weekly titration visits — dose changes, in person

    If medication is prescribed, your dose is increased gradually and reviewed at each visit by your doctor, alongside nutrition and lifestyle coaching — not an app deciding your dose for you.

  4. Between visits — WhatsApp, your core care channel

    Reminders, scheduling, coaching and progress check-ins all happen over WhatsApp for convenience. This channel never diagnoses or prescribes — it exists to support your doctor's care, not replace it.

  5. Ongoing maintenance — labs & check-ins continue

    As you progress, lab frequency and visit cadence adjust — your doctor continues to monitor and adjust your treatment for as long as you're with us.
